What gets written here, and what does not

These pieces cover the parts of learning yoga that a pose guide cannot hold: what a first month is like, how to think about props, and how to keep a practice going through a week that goes badly.

What you will not find here: claims that yoga treats, cures or prevents anything; weight-loss framing of any kind; calorie figures; before-and-after content; body-shape commentary; or diet, cleanse, fasting or detox material. Those things are common in this category and this site does not publish them. If an article ever appears here that was paid for by a brand, it will say so at the top of the page, above the first paragraph, before you read a word of it.

Corrections are welcome and get made. If something on this site is wrong, tell us and it will be fixed, with a dated note on the page saying what changed.

Health and safety

Everything published here is general movement education, not medical advice. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new physical practice, particularly if you are pregnant, recently injured, post-surgical or managing a health condition. Stop if you feel pain. Read the full disclaimer.
