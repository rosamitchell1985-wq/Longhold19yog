Work with us

Advertising, sponsored articles, affiliate partnerships and featured directory placement. What is available, how it is labeled, and the editorial rules that do not move.

The short version for prospective partners

We label everything paid. Sponsored articles say so above the first paragraph. Affiliate links carry a disclosure next to the link. Featured directory listings are marked as featured. We do not accept work from supplement, diet, cleanse, fasting or weight-loss businesses, and we do not publish anything that claims yoga treats a health condition. If those constraints do not fit the campaign, we are the wrong site and we would rather say that at the start.

Display advertising

Every page except the legal pages and the 404 page carries one clearly labeled display slot, placed below the main content of the page.

  • Placement. Below the primary content block. Never above the fold, never adjacent to navigation, never touching a button, never inside an instructional sequence.
  • Density. One slot per page. Content density is kept well above advertising density on every page of the site.
  • Layout stability. The slot has a reserved height at every breakpoint, so the page does not move when an ad loads.
  • Labeling. Each slot carries a visible "Advertisement" label above it and is visually distinct from the editorial content around it.
  • Consent. No advertising cookie is set before a visitor consents through the cookie banner. Visitors who reject non-essential cookies see non-personalized advertising only.
  • Formats we do not run. No pop-ups, no pop-unders, no interstitials on entry, no auto-playing audio or video, no auto-redirects, no auto-downloads, and nothing that interferes with the browser back button.

Advertising is bought through our ad partners rather than direct. For direct placement enquiries, use the form below.

Sponsored articles

We publish a small number of sponsored articles. The rules are the same for every one and they are not negotiable.

  • Labeled at the top. A sponsored article carries a clear label above the first paragraph identifying the sponsor by name. Not at the bottom, not in a footer, not in small print.
  • Written by us or reviewed by us. Copy provided by a sponsor is edited to the same standards as everything else here, including the removal of any health claim.
  • No health claims, at all. A sponsored article cannot state or imply that a product, pose, sequence or practice treats, cures, heals, relieves or prevents any condition. This applies to the sponsor's own product copy.
  • No weight-loss framing. No calorie figures, no fat-burning language, no body transformation, no before-and-after content, no toning claims.
  • Categories we decline. Supplements, diet programs, cleanses, detox products, fasting programs, weight-loss products or services, and any product marketed on a health outcome.
  • No fake urgency. No countdowns, no invented scarcity, no expiring discounts.
  • Editorial control stays here. Sponsors see the piece before publication and can correct factual errors about their own product. They do not have approval over the framing, the conclusions or the rest of the page.

Affiliate partnerships

We run affiliate links on physical practice equipment: mats, blocks, straps, blankets and bolsters. Nothing else.

  • Every affiliate link carries a disclosure immediately next to it, in the same block of content, not only in the site footer.
  • Every prop we recommend has a free household substitute listed alongside it, in the same paragraph or list. A hardback book is a block. A bathrobe belt is a strap.
  • Commission rates do not decide what appears, what is recommended first, or how a product is described.
  • We do not run affiliate links on anything ingested, on anything marketed on a health outcome, or on clothing.
  • We do not publish "best of" listicles built around affiliate revenue, and we do not publish product roundups that exist only to carry links.

The full FTC-compliant statement is on the Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure page.

Featured directory placement

A basic listing in the teacher directory is free, permanently, for any teacher or studio operating in the United States. Featured placement is paid, appears above the basic listings for its metropolitan area, includes a photograph and a longer description, and carries a visible "Featured placement" label wherever it appears.

Featured placement buys position and space. It does not buy an endorsement, a review or a rating, because we do not publish ratings for anyone. Nothing about a listing implies that Longhold Yoga has vetted the teacher, and the directory page says so plainly at the top.

Who reads this site

The audience is primarily in the United States and is weighted heavily toward people in their first year of practice: beginners looking up a pose by name, people building a home practice, and people who want written instruction rather than video.

The most-read pages are the beginner's guide, the pose library, the four sequences and the props guide. Traffic arrives mostly from search, on queries about specific poses and specific practice lengths.

We do not publish visitor numbers on this page, because a number here would be out of date within a month and unverifiable by you in any case. Current figures, verified through our analytics provider, are shared with prospective partners on request.

Why the rules are this strict

Yoga sits next to a large market in health claims, and a great deal of it is unsubstantiated. Publishing a claim that a pose relieves back pain, or that a breathing pattern lowers blood pressure, or that a sequence burns a certain number of calories, would be easy and would perform well.

We do not, for two reasons. The first is that those claims are not ours to make, because this is a movement education site written by teachers rather than clinicians. The second is that a site which makes them stops being trustworthy on everything else, including the parts we do know about, like where your back foot goes in Warrior II.

Partners who are comfortable with that constraint tend to be a good fit. Partners who are not usually find out quickly, which saves everybody time.
