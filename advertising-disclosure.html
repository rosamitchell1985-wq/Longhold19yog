Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Every way this site makes money, written out. Nothing here is hidden in a footer, and none of it changes what we recommend.

Effective date: 08/27/2026  ·  Last updated: 08/27/2026

The short version

Longhold Yoga earns money from display advertising, an optional paid membership, affiliate commissions on practice equipment, paid digital downloads, occasional labeled sponsored articles, and paid featured placement in the teacher directory. Affiliate links are disclosed next to the link itself. Sponsored articles are labeled at the top of the page. All prices are in US dollars.

1. FTC affiliate disclosure

Longhold Yoga participates in affiliate advertising programs. This site contains affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, Longhold Yoga may earn a commission, at no additional cost to you.

This disclosure is made in accordance with the United States Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255.

In addition to this page, we place a plain-language disclosure immediately beside every affiliate link on the site, in the same block of content, before you click it. We do not rely on this page alone, and we do not rely on the footer.

2. What we run affiliate links on

Physical practice equipment only: mats, blocks, straps, blankets and bolsters.

We do not run affiliate links on:

  • Anything ingested, including supplements, powders, teas and drinks.
  • Anything marketed on a health outcome.
  • Diet programs, cleanses, fasting programs or weight-loss products of any kind.
  • Clothing.
  • Yoga teacher training programs, retreats or certifications.

3. How products are chosen

  • Commission rates play no part. We do not know, and do not check, which retailer pays more before deciding what to recommend or in what order.
  • Every prop we recommend has a free household substitute listed alongside it, in the same paragraph or list. A hardback book is a block. A bathrobe belt is a strap. A folded bath towel is a blanket. Two firm pillows in a pillowcase is a bolster. Nothing on this site requires a purchase.
  • Sizing advice comes first, brand advice second. The props guide spends far more space on what size to get and what it does than on where to buy it, because that is the part that actually matters.
  • We do not publish affiliate listicles. No "best yoga mats of 2026" roundups, no comparison tables built to carry links, no product pages that exist only as a place to put affiliate URLs.

4. Display advertising

This site carries display advertising served by third-party advertising vendors, including Google. Advertising is how the free pose library, the free sequences and the free written guides are paid for.

How we place it:

  • One labeled slot per page, below the main content. Never above it in the fold.
  • No advertising slots on the legal pages or on the 404 page.
  • Slots never sit next to navigation, never touch a button, and never interrupt an instructional sequence.
  • Space is reserved at every breakpoint so nothing on the page moves when an ad loads.
  • Content density stays well above advertising density on every page.
  • No pop-ups, pop-unders, entry interstitials, auto-playing audio or video, auto-redirects or auto-downloads. Nothing on this site interferes with your browser's back button.

Advertising cookies are not set until you consent through the cookie banner. If you reject them, we request non-personalized advertising only. See the Cookie Policy for the detail and the Privacy Policy for the vendor list and opt-out links.

We do not control the content of third-party advertisements and are not responsible for the products or services advertised. If you see an ad on this site that makes a health claim or looks deceptive, please tell us so we can block it.

5. Membership

Membership costs $6.00 per month or $54.00 per year in US dollars. It removes advertising from every page and adds downloadable sequence PDFs, saved practice collections, printable pose cards, a practice log and early access to new guides.

Membership never puts instruction behind a wall. Nothing that is free to read today becomes paid later. Full terms are on the Pricing page and in section 7 of the Terms of Use.

6. Paid digital products

We sell downloadable PDF products: a structured four-week beginner program, sequence bundles and a printable pose card deck. Prices are listed in US dollars on the Pricing page. These are our own products, so there is no third party and no commission involved; we simply earn the money directly.

7. Sponsored content

We publish a small number of sponsored articles, paid for by a brand.

  • Every sponsored article carries a clear label at the top of the page, above the first paragraph, naming the sponsor. Not at the bottom. Not in the footer. Not in small print.
  • Copy provided by a sponsor is edited to the same standards as everything else here, including removal of any health claim.
  • A sponsored article cannot state or imply that a product, pose, sequence or practice treats, cures, heals, relieves or prevents any condition.
  • No weight-loss framing, calorie figures, body transformation or before-and-after content is permitted in sponsored work.
  • We do not accept sponsorship from supplement, diet, cleanse, fasting or weight-loss businesses.
  • Sponsors may correct factual errors about their own product before publication. They do not have approval over the framing or the conclusions.

Our full standards for sponsors are on the Work With Us page.

8. The teacher directory

A basic listing in the teacher directory is free and always will be. Featured placement is paid, appears above the basic listings for its metropolitan area, and carries a visible "Featured placement" label wherever it appears.

Featured placement buys position and space. It does not buy an endorsement, a review or a rating. Longhold Yoga does not vet, endorse, certify or supervise anyone in the directory, and we publish no ratings for anyone, because we have no way to assess teaching quality at a distance.

9. Testimonials, reviews and credentials

This site publishes no reviews, no star ratings and no testimonials, because we have not collected any that we can verify. Where a page currently shows a template for a listing, a teacher biography or a credential, it is marked in the page source with a placeholder notice and is written as a template rather than as a claim about a real person.

We will not publish an invented review, an invented credential or an invented endorsement. If real ones appear here in future, they will be real, attributed and verifiable.

10. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your email address or any personal information. See the Privacy Policy.
  • We do not make health, medical or therapeutic claims anywhere on this site, in editorial or in paid placements.
  • We do not publish weight-loss, calorie, body-transformation or diet content.
  • We do not use fake urgency, fake scarcity, countdown timers or expiring offers.
  • We do not publish clickbait headlines or content designed only to carry advertising.
  • We do not accept payment to change a written recommendation.

11. Questions

If anything on this page is unclear, or if you think a disclosure is missing from somewhere on the site, tell us and we will fix it: hello@longholdyoga.com, or Longhold Yoga, 1412 SE Cortland Avenue, Suite 3, Portland, OR 97202, United States, or +1 (503) 555-0147.
