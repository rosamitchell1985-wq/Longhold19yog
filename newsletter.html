The Monday Sequence

One short practice and one written note, in your inbox every Monday morning. Free, no account needed to read anything on this site, and one click unsubscribes you for good.

What arrives

Every Monday, one email containing exactly two things.

  • One practice, between five and twenty minutes, written out in full in the email with hold times. You do not have to click through to use it.
  • One note, usually three or four paragraphs, about something specific: a cue that changes a pose, why a prop goes where it goes, or a common misreading of an instruction.

That is the whole format and it does not change. No round-ups, no affiliate blasts, no product launches dressed up as content. If a given week's email contains a paid placement, it is labeled as a paid placement inside the email.

What does not arrive

  • No daily emails. One per week, on Monday.
  • No health, medical or therapeutic claims, in the newsletter or anywhere else on this site.
  • No weight-loss, calorie, body-transformation or diet content of any kind.
  • No pressure sequences, countdown offers or fake deadlines.

Email practices, stated plainly

This is written out in full because the law requires some of it and because you should be able to read it before you hand over an address, not afterwards.

  • Who is sending it. Every email comes from Longhold Yoga, from the address hello@longholdyoga.com. The sender name and the subject line will always accurately describe what is inside.
  • Our physical mailing address appears in the footer of every single email: Longhold Yoga, 1412 SE Cortland Avenue, Suite 3, Portland, OR 97202, United States.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email carries a working one-click unsubscribe link in the footer. It takes effect immediately, with no login, no survey, and no "are you sure" step. You can also reply to any email with the word unsubscribe, or email hello@longholdyoga.com and we will remove you by hand.
  • What we store. Your email address, your first name if you gave one, your practice-length answer if you gave one, and the dates you opened or clicked. Nothing else.
  • We do not sell or rent your address, to anyone, for any purpose. If a sponsored placement appears in an email, the sponsor receives aggregate numbers only and never your address.
  • How long we keep it. Until you unsubscribe, plus thirty days, after which the record is deleted. If you have not opened an email in eighteen months we remove you automatically.

Full detail is in the Privacy Policy. If you are in California, the CCPA and CPRA section sets out your rights and how to exercise them.

If you would rather not give an email address

Then do not. Nothing on this site is behind a signup. Every pose guide, every sequence and every article is fully readable and fully printable without an account. The newsletter is a convenience, not a gate.
