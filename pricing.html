Pricing and membership

The pose library, the sequences and every written guide are free and stay free. Membership removes the advertising and adds things you can download, save and print. All prices in US dollars.

The rule we hold ourselves to

Membership never puts instruction behind a wall. Nothing that is free today becomes paid tomorrow. If a pose guide, a sequence or an article is readable now, it will be readable without an account for as long as this site exists. Membership pays for the ads to go away and for the material that takes extra work to produce.

How this site is funded

Six revenue sources, all of them disclosed. There is no seventh.

  • Display advertising. Every page except the legal pages and the 404 page carries one labeled ad slot, placed below the main content. Ads never sit above the content in the fold, never touch a button or the navigation, and the space is reserved in the layout so nothing moves when an ad loads. Advertising cookies are not set until you consent to them.
  • Membership. $6 per month or $54 per year in USD, described above.
  • Affiliate commissions on mats, blocks, straps, blankets and bolsters. If you buy through one of our links we earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Every affiliate link carries a disclosure right next to it, not only in the footer, and every prop recommendation on this site lists a free household substitute alongside it.
  • Paid digital products, listed above.
  • Sponsored content and brand partnerships. Any article paid for by a brand is labeled as sponsored at the top of the page, above the first paragraph. We do not publish sponsored content that makes health claims, and we do not accept sponsorship from supplement, diet, cleanse or weight-loss businesses.
  • The teacher directory. A basic listing is free. A featured placement is paid and is marked as featured wherever it appears. Details here.

The full version, including the FTC-required affiliate language, is on the Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure page.
