Privacy policy

What Longhold Yoga collects, why, who else is involved, how long it is kept, and how to make us stop.

Effective date: 08/27/2026  ·  Last updated: 08/27/2026

This policy is written to be read rather than skimmed past. If anything in it is unclear, email hello@longholdyoga.com and we will explain it in plain terms.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signup: your email address, and optionally your first name and how long you have been practicing.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, chosen topic, message, and phone number if you provide one.
  • Teacher directory submission: business or teacher name, city and state, email address, optional phone number, and the description you write.
  • Partnership enquiries: name, company, work email address, enquiry type and message.
  • Membership, when it opens: name, email address and billing details. Card numbers are handled entirely by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers or seen by us.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages requested, and the date and time of each request. Kept for 30 days for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Analytics, only if you consent: pages viewed, approximate location at city level, device category, and how you arrived at the site.
  • Advertising, only if you consent: identifiers set by advertising vendors, described in section 5.

What we do not collect

We do not collect health information, injury history, medical conditions, pregnancy status, body measurements, weight or any biometric data. This site has no fields for any of that and no interest in it. Please do not include health information in a contact form message; if you do, we will delete it and reply asking you to speak with a qualified healthcare professional instead.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. We use four categories, and only the first is set before you choose.

  • Strictly necessary. Always on. Records your cookie choice so the banner does not reappear on every page, and keeps forms and sessions working. Cannot be switched off because the site cannot function without them.
  • Preferences. Remembers settings such as a paused breath pacer or a chosen filter, so the site behaves consistently between visits.
  • Analytics. Off until you consent. Counts page views and how visitors move between pages, in aggregate.
  • Advertising. Off until you consent. Set by advertising vendors, including Google, to serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising.

No analytics or advertising cookie is set before you make a choice in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer of every page. Full detail, including each cookie's name, purpose and lifespan, is in the Cookie Policy.

5. Third parties, including Google

We use a small number of service providers. Each receives only what it needs.

  • Google and other advertising vendors. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info, and you can opt out of many vendors at once at the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page. Where advertising consent has not been given, we request non-personalized advertising only.
  • Analytics provider. Aggregate usage measurement, loaded only after consent. IP addresses are truncated before storage where the provider supports it.
  • Email service provider. Stores your subscription record and sends the newsletter. Sees your email address, first name and engagement data.
  • Payment processor, when membership opens. Handles card details directly. We never see or store a card number.
  • Web host and content delivery network. Processes server logs and serves the pages and images.
  • Font and image delivery. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts and photographs from an image content delivery network. These services receive your IP address as a technical necessity of delivering the file.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we never share your email address with advertisers or sponsors.

6. How long we keep things

  • Newsletter subscription: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days. If you have not opened an email in 18 months we remove you automatically.
  • Contact form messages: 24 months from the date received, then deleted.
  • Directory listings: for as long as the listing is published, plus 12 months after removal.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Membership and billing records: 7 years, as required for US tax and accounting purposes.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to personal data is limited to people who need it to do their job. Payment card details never touch our servers. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email hello@longholdyoga.com and we will delete it promptly. If we discover such information ourselves, we delete it without waiting to be asked.

Nothing on this site should be used to instruct a child in a physical practice without the supervision of a qualified teacher and the involvement of a parent or guardian.

9. California residents: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. What categories of personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, and which categories of third party received it.
  • Right to access a copy of the specific pieces of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions such as tax records.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as that term is defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to non-discrimination. Exercising any of these rights will not result in a worse price, a worse service or any different treatment.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Longhold Yoga does not sell personal information for money. However, allowing advertising cookies may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CPRA. You can opt out of that in either of two ways, both of which are free and take effect immediately:

  • Open the cookie settings and turn Advertising off, or choose Reject non-essential. This link also appears in the footer of every page on this site.
  • Email hello@longholdyoga.com with "Do Not Sell or Share" in the subject line, or write to the mailing address in section 1.

We honor the Global Privacy Control browser signal as a valid opt-out request where your browser sends one.

How to make a California request

Email hello@longholdyoga.com with "CCPA request" in the subject line, or write to the mailing address in section 1. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by a further 45 days where necessary, in which case we will tell you why. We may need to verify your identity, usually by asking you to reply from the email address associated with the data. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf with written permission.

Categories collected in the last 12 months

Identifiers (email address, name, IP address); internet activity (pages viewed, referring page); commercial information (membership purchases, when membership opens); and, for directory listings, professional information you choose to submit. Sources are you, your browser, and our service providers. Purposes are set out in section 3. Categories disclosed for a business purpose: identifiers and internet activity, to the service providers listed in section 5.

10. Visitors from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

This site is aimed at visitors in the United States, but it is reachable from anywhere. If you are in the EEA or the UK, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing that already happened.

Data is processed in the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, our providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved transfer mechanism.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@longholdyoga.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

11. Other US state privacy laws

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and Montana, have similar rights to access, correct, delete and port their personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same contact routes set out in section 9 and tell us which state you reside in. Where a state provides an appeal process for a refused request, we will explain it in our response.

12. How to exercise any right on this page

One email covers all of it: hello@longholdyoga.com. Tell us what you want and which state or country you are in. You can also write to the mailing address in section 1, or call +1 (503) 555-0147 during Pacific business hours. There is no fee.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the "last updated" date at the top and, for material changes, place a notice on the site for at least 30 days. Where a change requires it, we will ask for your consent again. Previous versions are available on request.

14. Contact

Questions, requests and complaints about privacy: hello@longholdyoga.com, or Longhold Yoga, 1412 SE Cortland Avenue, Suite 3, Portland, OR 97202, United States, or +1 (503) 555-0147. We reply within two business days and resolve requests within the timeframes set out above.
